How to use the Reads.

Use one when you’re sitting inside the question it’s asking. Use two when the result of the first surprises you. Use the COPPSE Read at the start of a transformation, and again at the half-way point — the pattern of which dimensions hold and which slip will tell you more than any progress report.

Every response is held in confidence. Nothing is stored on a server until you choose to send the results through — and even then, only to my inbox. If you’d like the read translated into a conversation about what to do next, that’s included and free.
