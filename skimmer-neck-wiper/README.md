# Manual neck wiper lid. Bubble Magus Curve 5 / Curve A5 / Extreme 5

A manual copy of the Bubble Magus ACS neck cleaner. Turn the thumb dial on the lid and a single rubber strip sweeps the inside of the 55 mm foam neck. A second thumb knob under the lid sets how hard the rubber presses on the wall.

The lid is the stock Curve A5 lid from Printables model 182898, modified. Its rim groove, vent holes and 85 mm foam ring are kept. The drip cone is removed and a bearing is added. The stock STL and the modified lid are not committed here because they derive from that Printables model. Put `stock_curve_a5_lid.stl` next to the .scad to rebuild the lid.

No supports needed. 4 walls, 40% infill on the rotor and carriage.

## Hardware
- 1x M4 x 20 hex bolt and 1x M4 hex nut, nylon or 316 stainless. Plain steel will rust in the cup.
- 2x 15 mm pieces of 1.75 mm filament as pins.

## Rubber
Buy food grade silicone rubber sheet, 1/16 in or 1.5 mm thick, 50 to 60 Shore A. A 12 x 12 in sheet is plenty for years of spares.

Why silicone:
- It is inert in saltwater and does not leach into the tank.
- It stays flexible and does not harden or crack the way neoprene and natural rubber do.
- It will not scratch acrylic.

EPDM sheet at the same thickness is a fine second choice. Avoid neoprene, natural rubber and anything sold as recycled rubber.

Cut a strip 8 mm wide by 58 mm long with a sharp knife and a steel ruler. Clip the bottom outer corner at 45 degrees so it slides into the neck. If you own a printer that runs TPU 85A, you can print blade.stl instead.

## Measure one thing
`neck_top_drop` is the distance from the lid underside down to the top edge of the neck. I set it to 22 mm as a placeholder. Measure yours and re-export the carriage and blade if it is different.

## Assembly
1. Press the M4 nut up into the hex pocket under the carriage pad.
2. Push the rubber strip up into the slot from the bottom until it stops. Push a filament pin through the hole near the bottom to lock it.
3. Push the rotor up through the lid from underneath. The collar stops against the boss.
4. Press the dial onto the square key, line up the holes, push a filament pin through. Trim flush.
5. Press the knob onto the M4 bolt head. Drop the bolt down through the arm slot and thread it into the carriage nut. The rib on the carriage rides in the slot.
6. Fit the lid on the cup.

## Setting the wipe
1. Lift the lid. Loosen the knob a half turn.
2. Slide the carriage out until the rubber just touches the neck wall, then go about 1.5 mm further. The ticks on the arm are 1 mm apart.
3. Tighten the knob and turn the dial. You want the rubber to bend slightly and drag, not fold flat.
4. Turn the dial a few times a day or before you empty the cup.

The vent holes are required. A sealed lid will push foam back down.
